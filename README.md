# PvZ Fusion English for Android

An English fan translation of Plants vs. Zombies Fusion, using the Blooms community's PC translations with Android UI improvements.

## Download 4.1 pre-release 1

[Download the English APK](https://github.com/silvershadowkat/pvzf-android-translation/releases/download/v4.1-release-1/PvZ-Fusion-4.1-English-prerelease1.apk)

One APK, with English text and artwork, Original and Blooms music choices, and the official online Magic Plantbrush. ## Install or update

1. Back up your saves, just in case.
2. Download and install the APK. If you already use the Standard English app, install over it without uninstalling. Progress, extra save slots and settings stay in place.
3. Open the game and continue playing.

The Chinese app and the older AI app keep separate saves. If you are moving from either one, follow the [save transfer guide](https://github.com/silvershadowkat/pvzf-android-translation/blob/v4.1-release-1/docs/PROJECT-BACKGROUND.md#moving-saves-without-restoring-old-translation-files). Keep your old app until your transferred saves load correctly.

## What's new

- Updated to Fusion 4.1 with reviewed Blooms translations and fixes for reported Gods card and almanac text.
- More English text in Odyssey: Evolved, Super Stacking and Magic Plantbrush, plus improved almanac text sizing.
- Reworked music playback and balanced soundtrack volume.
- Restored separate Music and Screen controls, and fixed settings buttons moving when pressed.
- Magic Plantbrush uses the game's official online recognition service. It needs an internet connection; there is no separate offline AI APK for this release.

You can choose Original or Blooms music in Settings. Close and reopen the game after changing the soundtrack. The Screen control switches between the default view and Stretch.

Some specialized terms remain Chinese where a verified translation is unavailable. Longer sessions and more device types still need testing. If you find an issue, [report it](https://github.com/silvershadowkat/pvzf-android-translation/issues) with a screenshot, your device model and the mode you were playing.
