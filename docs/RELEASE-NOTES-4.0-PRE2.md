# Android 4.0 English prerelease 2

- Fixed The Gods Almanac opening empty with missing tabs and cards. Restored internal references and added regression checks.
- Corrected 14 plant descriptions against official 4.0 text while keeping PC names. **Nugget-shroom attacks every 2.25 seconds; reproduction remains 20 seconds.** Coffee Bean's "exempt" wording was verified and left unchanged.
- Used **context-aware AI translation** for generic Almanac tabs, search, paths, level labels, unlock prompts, availability requirements, and clear basic effects.
- Used **God Coins** consistently for the mode's currency, including balances, costs and rewards. This is an approved provisional English name, chosen over awkward literal wording such as "the gods' coins". Future verified PC wording takes priority.
- Left unconfirmed tactic names, specialized effects and unclear descriptions in Chinese. Even familiar words can be ability names, so guessing could confuse players or conflict with PC guides.
- Retained the previous English text, presentation fixes and **17 PC music tracks**. Gameplay code, saves and permissions are unchanged. Unspecified mode glitches and lingering zombie sprites have not been reproduced or claimed fixed.
- **48 tests passed**, plus 315 runtime-format checks, catalog-reference checks, APK structure, signature and alignment checks. The maintainer confirmed the Almanac and Nugget-shroom fixes and approved the final rev 3 APK for publication unchanged. This remains a **prerelease**.
