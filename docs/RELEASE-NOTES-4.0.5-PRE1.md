# Android 4.0.5 English prerelease 1

## Game update

- Updated to the official Android 4.0.5 release.
- Retained verified English translations, fonts, presentation fixes and 17 music replacements.

## Translation updates

- Added the latest available PC English names and descriptions, including new plants and updated modifier wording.
- Checked changed Chinese descriptions and corrected outdated damage, health and modifier values.
- Updated the changelog, drawing and level-editor controls, and related instructions.
- Replaced older assisted wording with usable current PC English where available.

## Testing

- The tested APK is published unchanged.
- An S26 Ultra smoke test was generally smooth, with no obvious new issues reported. This does not cover every mode or device.
- Automated tests, formatting, APK integrity, signing and alignment checks passed. Native Android code remains unchanged.

## Known translation gaps

- The Gods Almanac still has untranslated areas, especially tactics. Specialized text without verified English remains Chinese.
- Two ambiguous plant descriptions and puzzle labels that also serve as loading keys remain Chinese.
- Magic Plantbrush UI is translated where present; full Android gameplay support is unverified.

Install the APK below over your existing English installation. Back up your saves before upgrading. Do not restore old translation or hot-patch files.
