# PvZ Fusion 4.1 English Android: Pre-release 1

One download: **PvZ-Fusion-4.1-English-prerelease1.apk**.

## Updating

Back up your saves, then install over your existing Standard English app without uninstalling. Progress, extra save slots and settings are preserved.

The Chinese app and the older AI app have separate storage. To move from either one, use the [save transfer guide](https://github.com/silvershadowkat/pvzf-android-translation/blob/v4.1-release-1/docs/PROJECT-BACKGROUND.md#moving-saves-without-restoring-old-translation-files). Keep the old app until your transferred saves load correctly.

## Changes

- Updated to official Android 4.1 and reviewed Blooms 4.1 English data. Existing English artwork and credits are retained where the original assets match.
- Fixed reported Gods card and almanac gaps, including Ascend, Disintegration, Penetration, Toxicity, Impact and Firecracker text. Added verified records for Artsy Sunflower, Ball Cactus, SP Calamity-shroom and SP Avarice-shroom.
- Expanded English text in Odyssey: Evolved, Super Stacking, the 4.1 announcement and Magic Plantbrush. Restored Android almanac text sizing and fixed an incorrect fusion-discovery banner.
- Reworked music playback using Unity's original audio system, balanced soundtrack levels and released inactive decoded tracks. Original and Blooms remain selectable in Settings. Close and reopen the game after changing the soundtrack.
- Restored separate Music and Screen controls. Screen switches between the default view and Stretch. Fixed the four settings buttons that moved when pressed.
- Retained touch access to Projectile Info and customizable button positioning.
- Enabled 4.1's official online Magic Plantbrush recognition and removed the outdated "Untranslated" label. Recognition needs internet and depends on the official service. This release has no offline AI variant.

Gameplay terminology follows reviewed PC translations. Context Aware AI assists with clear prose and generic UI using established terms; unverified specialized names remain Chinese. Longer sessions and more device types still need testing.

The final r4 candidate passed S26 checks for the affected settings buttons. Earlier checks covered text layout, an existing Super Stacking save, the music popup and paused-settings navigation. The tested APK is published unchanged.

Thanks to the Fusion team, the Blooms translation community, Joseph Franci, aha and all testers. Thanks to ClarkStacks for Projectile Info feedback and S.O.R.O.B for music choice.
