# PvZ Fusion 4.1 English Android: Pre-release 2

Choose your download:

- **Standard:** `PvZ-Fusion-4.1-English-prerelease2.apk`. Uses the official online Magic Plantbrush service.
- **Offline AI:** `PvZ-Fusion-4.1-English-prerelease2-AI.apk`. Restores local plant recognition in the separate ENAI app. Uses more memory and a plant-only catalog that does not include every 4.1 plant.

## Changes

- Fixed Plant Info stats merging or disappearing, and translated missing headings.
- Added reviewed plant descriptions and drawing labels.
- Made Customize UI Positions labels a consistent size.
- Reduced avoidable AI work. Once loaded, the offline recognizer stays ready between modes while the game runs.

## Updating

Back up your saves, then install Standard over EN or Offline AI over ENAI without uninstalling. EN and ENAI keep separate saves. For transfers from another app, use the [save guide](https://github.com/silvershadowkat/pvzf-android-translation/blob/v4.1-release-2/docs/PROJECT-BACKGROUND.md#moving-saves-without-restoring-old-translation-files).

For a larger field view, set Camera Zoom to 5. Music choices are unchanged; reopen the game after switching soundtracks.

Checked on the S26 with saved files preserved. Thanks to the Fusion team, Blooms translators and everyone testing and reporting issues.
