# PvZ Fusion 4.1 English Android: Pre-release 3

- Fixed runtime translations and settings controls disappearing after Android restarts the game.
- Fixed Original intro music playing before a Blooms track and restored the game's roof-track preloading for the selected soundtrack.
- Added missing plant and zombie descriptions, card effects and a changelog translation.
- Preserved the Plant Info stat fixes and the Original/Stretch screen control.

**Standard EN** uses the official online Magic Plantbrush service. **Offline ENAI** uses local plant recognition and keeps its model ready while the game runs.

Install over the matching EN or ENAI app without uninstalling. Your saves stay in place.

Some new 4.1 names remain Chinese until verified English names are available. The intermittent Starbound music repeat still needs more feedback.
